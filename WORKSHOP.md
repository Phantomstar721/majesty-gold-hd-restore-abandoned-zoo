# Restore Abandoned Zoo

Complete one of Majesty Gold HD's most intriguing abandoned features and put
the kingdom's monsters on display.

Restore Abandoned Zoo adds a fully playable three-level Zoo. Place private
Capture Flags on hostile monsters, let your heroes subdue and escort successful
captures, and manage the resulting menagerie as a new part of your kingdom.

## Required launcher

This mod requires Majesty Mod Manager:

https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793024054

Subscribe to both items, run `Majesty Mod Manager.exe` from the manager's
Workshop folder, select Restore Abandoned Zoo on the **Merge** tab, choose
**Prepare Selected Mods**, and use **Launch Majesty** inside the manager.

Do not enable the raw Zoo package directly in Majesty's mod selector. The stock
executable does not provide the private panels, flag placement, visitor
rendering, and follower support required by the mod.

## Features

- Build and upgrade the restored Zoo through three levels
- Capture ordinary or custom hostile monsters without per-monster scripting
- Store 4, 6, or 8 visitors depending on Zoo level
- View monster icons, health, names, actions, and 1-8 Threat Ranks
- Earn 40 gold per Threat Rank per captive every minute
- Balance valuable exhibits against a 5 percent per-minute breakout risk
- Release a selected monster with **Tame Beast** as a friendly Palace guardian
- Toggle hero rentals so wealthy heroes can purchase a captured follower after
  completing their normal shopping
- Keep captives secured when the Zoo takes damage; destroying it releases every
  survivor at full health as a hostile monster
- Use original Zoo art together with custom Zoo panels, Capture Flag art,
  cursor art, buttons, and minimap markers
- Play with Original Majesty and Northern Expansion content

Threat Rank is derived generically from each monster's stock combat experience
bounty. Taming costs 500 gold per rank; hero rentals cost 100 gold per rank.
Capture odds retain the abandoned stock Zoo formula and cap at 95 percent.

Restore Abandoned Zoo is built as a merge-compatible package. Majesty Mod
Manager can combine it with other supported custom content without relying on
load order or replacing the original Palace reward system.
