RESTORE ABANDONED ZOO

This mod requires Majesty Mod Manager:
https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793024054

Run Majesty Mod Manager.exe from its Workshop folder, select Restore Abandoned
Zoo on the Merge tab, choose Prepare Selected Mods, and launch Majesty through
the manager.

Do not enable this raw package directly in Majesty's mod selector.
