THE ROYAL MENAGERIE

This mod requires Majesty Mod Manager:
https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793024054

Run Majesty Mod Manager.exe from its Workshop folder, select The Royal
Menagerie on the Merge tab, choose Prepare Selected Mods, and launch Majesty
through the manager.

Do not enable this raw package directly in Majesty's mod selector.
